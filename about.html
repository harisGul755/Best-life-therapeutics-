About us

We want you to get the care you deserve.

A modern, team-based approach to mental health care.

Congratulations — the fact that you're here says a lot. Something has motivated you to seek support, whether for yourself, your family or with a partner. The step you're about to take is life changing, and we look forward to going along with you on your journey.

We take a team approach: we're the mental health professionals, but you're the life professional and team lead. Working together gives you the tools to live your best life.

Our mission

High-quality, affordable therapy for a diverse community.

We serve people who may not qualify for subsidized public services but still face financial barriers to care. Our vision is a world where everyone has access to quality mental health care — and our clients leave with skills to navigate life's challenges.
