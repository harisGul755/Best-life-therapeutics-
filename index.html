Who we are

Quality mental health care shouldn't depend on your budget.

01

Care you can actually access

We created this practice for people who may not qualify for subsidized public services but still need affordable, high-quality therapy.

02

You are the life professional

We come in as the mental health professional, but you are the team lead — the expert on your own life. Together we build the tools you need.

03

Evidence-based and practical

Structured therapy using CBT, trauma-informed care and somatic approaches, focused on tools you can use in daily life.

04

In person or from home

Meet at our Reston office near the Wiehle–Reston East Metro, or by secure telehealth anywhere in Virginia.

What we treat

Practical support for the things you're carrying.

Anxiety and stress, depression, trauma and PTSD, grief and loss, and major life transitions.
